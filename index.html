About Me

Know Me More

Hello, I'm Hafza Noor

I am a mathematics researcher at Bahauddin Zakariya University, Multan, working on trustworthy deep learning for brain tumor MRI: from 2D tumor classification to 3D multimodal tumor segmentation, supervised by Dr. Fiza Zafar.

I care about models that can be trusted, not just models that score well. In my MRI work I audited the benchmark for train–test leakage, measured calibration, and added Grad-CAM explanations and human-review escalation. My mathematics background (optimization, statistics and numerical analysis) shapes how I design and evaluate models.

Alongside research, I work as a freelance data analyst building dashboards and data pipelines in Power BI, SQL and Excel.

Explainable Brain Tumor MRI Classification with Uncertainty-Aware Decision Support

Fine-tuned MobileNetV2 classifies brain MRI into glioma, meningioma, pituitary tumor or no tumor, with Grad-CAM explanations and human-review escalation for low-confidence scans.

95.2%accuracy, 1,600 test images
92.8%on duplicate-free subset
36.4%test images leaked from training
  • Leakage audit with perceptual hashing found 582 duplicated test images, so I report the clean score.
  • Error analysis: glioma is the weakest class (recall 82%).
  • Calibration: temperature scaling (T = 1.83) cut negative log-likelihood by 15%.

3D Multimodal Brain Tumor Segmentation

A lightweight 3D U-Net segments whole tumor, tumor core and enhancing tumor from four MRI modalities, as groundwork for segmentation in real-world clinical MRI. Supervised by Dr. Fiza Zafar (BZU).

484MRI volumes (BraTS-derived)
4modalities: FLAIR, T1w, T1gd, T2w
338/73/73patient-level split, no leakage
  • Per-modality z-score normalization on brain voxels; Dice loss on 128³ patches.
  • Gradio prototype: upload a NIfTI scan, get region volumes and slice overlays.
