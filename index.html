Research & AI Projects

Recent work in computer vision and trustworthy AI

Published code · Live demo

Explainable Brain Tumor MRI Classification with Uncertainty-Aware Decision Support

MobileNetV2 fine-tuned in PyTorch to classify brain MRI into glioma, meningioma, pituitary tumor and no tumor. Grad-CAM heatmaps explain each prediction, low-confidence cases are escalated for human review, and a retrieval step (MiniLM + FAISS) feeds an LLM that writes a short educational report.

95.2%accuracy (1,600 test images)
92.8%on duplicate-free subset
0.951macro F1 (full test set)

A perceptual-hash audit found 582 test images (36.4%) duplicated in training, so I report the clean-subset score as the honest estimate. Error analysis flagged glioma as the weakest class (recall 82%), and temperature scaling (T = 1.83) cut negative log-likelihood by 15%.

PyTorch · Grad-CAM · FAISS · LangChain · Gradio

Ongoing research

3D Multimodal Brain Tumor Segmentation

Supervised by Dr. Fiza Zafar (BZU), toward segmentation in real-world MRI data. A lightweight 3D U-Net segments whole tumor, tumor core and enhancing tumor from four MRI modalities (FLAIR, T1w, T1gd, T2w) on 484 volumes from the Medical Segmentation Decathlon (BraTS-derived). A patient-level split (338 / 73 / 73) prevents train–test leakage. Next step: extension to real-world clinical MRI.

  • Brain-voxel z-score normalization per modality
  • Soft Dice loss, 128³ patch training on a Tesla T4 GPU
  • Gradio prototype: upload a NIfTI volume, get region volumes, slice overlays and related textbook passages

PyTorch · 3D U-Net · NiBabel · FAISS · Gradio

Undergraduate research · Seminar

Feature Selection for Tumor Classification using the Artificial Bee Colony Algorithm

Feature subset selection on tumor classification data formulated as an optimization problem minimizing classification error and solved with the ABC swarm-intelligence metaheuristic, compared with traditional methods on accuracy, convergence and computational cost. Supervised by Dr. Fiza Zafar (grade 4.00/4.00); presented at a CASPAM departmental seminar, 2025.
